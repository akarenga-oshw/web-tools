akarengaweb tools

Work with a board from the browser. Nothing to install; open in Chrome or Edge.

Renesas RA

renesas-ra-flasher

Writes flash over the standard boot firmware. Verified on RA4M1.

Any board with a serial port

usb-time-sync

Sets a board's RTC from the host clock, then reports the drift in ppm.

Both use Web Serial, which Firefox, Safari and Android do not implement. Everything happens between the browser and the board; nothing is sent to a server.
